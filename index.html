🔒 ACID Transactional Rules

All registration operations run in transaction.atomic() with row-level locks (select_for_update()):

  • Prerequisites Check: Enforces passed prerequisites.
  • Time Slot Clash Guard: Prevents schedule conflicts.
  • Capacity Cap: Rejects registration on full sections.
  • Semester Credit Limit: Max 18 credits per term.
  • No Double Registrations: Duplicate blocks in same term.
